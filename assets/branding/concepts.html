Engineering Lab — Emblem Concepts

Six simpler directions. Compare the silhouette first, then choose the identity worth refining.

01 · Letterform

Monogram

A compact E/L construction. Direct, memorable and strongest as a standalone brand mark.

02 · Framework

Keystone

A single structural frame with an abstract E. Suggests engineered systems and dependable foundations.

03 · Systems

Core Node

One central form with six restrained connections. Communicates computing and interconnected disciplines.

04 · Architecture

Structure

Two interlocking right angles create a precise E/L-like architectural symbol.

05 · Scientific method

Orbit

A minimal coordinate system and central point. A subtle reference to measurement, modelling and science.

06 · Geometry

Isometric

A clean three-dimensional form built from a few axes. Suggests design, physical systems and computation.
